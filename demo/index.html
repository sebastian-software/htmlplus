A regular form, with a little more vocabulary.
Every control participates through the browser’s form APIs. Specialized fields keep a real text input inside, while their normalized value goes into the form.
This preview checks ten IBAN country formats and their check digits, plus BIC structure. It does not verify bank accounts, BIC registration, or authentication codes.
